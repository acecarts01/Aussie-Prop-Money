# Pricing & Value Map — 2026-09-26

**Rule (client decision 2026-09-26, supersedes the 2026-09-14 "+5% charm rounding" pass): price = 20% of the prop face value the product carries.** Every currency-styled product now shows a flat **5× face value** multiplier (pay $1, receive $5 in prop face value). Face value is the printed value of the prop notes in the pack — it is never a real-currency value; every note is reduced-scale and marked NOT LEGAL TENDER, and this pricing rule does not change that compliance status in any way.

This is a full re-price, not an adjustment — the 2026-09-14 pass moved prices by single-digit percentages; this one moves every currency product's price by roughly 15×–420× (the ratio varies because the *old* prices weren't set as a percentage of face value at all — they were novelty-item production-cost pricing). `docs/pricing-2026-09-14.md` / `.json` are kept for historical reference only; they no longer reflect live prices.

Non-currency products (accessories, confetti, leis, display cases, personalised novelty pieces, kids play money) have no `faceValue` field and are **not** affected by this rule — they keep their existing individual pricing.

## Downstream effects checked

- `src/lib/value.js` (`valueReturn()`, `cartFaceValue()`, `yieldLadder()`) reads `product.price` / `product.faceValue` directly — no hardcoded numbers there, so the 5× multiplier and cart face-value totals update automatically from the repriced data.
- `SITE.orderRules.minOrder` raised to $350 the same day (separate client decision) — with these new prices, nearly every single currency product clears the minimum on its own; multi-item carts are no longer the norm for reaching it.
- The blog post `how-much-does-prop-money-cost-in-australia` (`POSTS` in `src/config/site.js`) had specific old-price examples ("$61.90", "mid-$20s to low-$30s", "$135 up to $240") rewritten to describe the new 20%-of-face-value rule instead of citing figures that are now wrong.
- No other blog post, FAQ, or page was found hardcoding a specific old dollar price (checked via full-text search across `src/`) — everything else derives prices dynamically from `PRODUCTS`.
